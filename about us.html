Our History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 and offers training courses in Durban. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals and have completed both the six-month professional Development programmes and the six-week practical skills courses.

Vision

To South Africa's leading academy for thr pet care and training, building a compassionate nation where every pet is understood, well-cared for and cherished.

Our Mission

Pawsitive Pet Academy exists to empowerpet owners, famallies, aspiring professionals, and future entrepreneurs through expert-led and accessible education in pet care, training, and business skills. Since 2023, the academy has equipped hundreds of graduates through practical six-week courses and comprehensive six-month programmes offered in Durban and beyond.

Our Goals

  • To educate
  • equip pet owners
  • aspiring professionals with proper animal care and behaviouraltraining techniques
  • To create meaningful employment
  • To create business opportunities for individual who are passionate about working with animals
  • To deliver a seamless, user centred digital experince through the website and mobile application
  • To connect more learners with life-changing courses
