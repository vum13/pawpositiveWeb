Our History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 and offers training courses in Durban. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals and have completed both the six-month professional Development programmes and the six-week practical skills courses.

Vision

To be South Africa's leading academy for pet care and training, building a compassionate nation where every pet is understood, well-cared for and cherished.

Our Mission

Pawsitive Pet Academy exists to empower pet owners, families, aspiring professionals, and future entrepreneurs through expert- led and accessible education in pet care, training, and business skills. Since 2023, the academy has equipped hundreds of graduates through practical six-week short courses and comprehensive six-month programmes offered in Durban and beyond.

Our Goals

  • To eduvate
  • Equip pet owner
  • Aspiring professionals with proper animal care and behavioural training techniques
  • To create meaningful employmenet
  • To create business opportunities for individaul who are passionate about working with animals
  • To deliver a seamless, user centred digita experience through the website and mobile applicaton
  • To connect more leaners with life-changing courses
