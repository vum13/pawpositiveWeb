Our History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 and offers training courses in Durban. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals have completed both the six-month Professional Development Programmes and the six-week Practical Skills Courses.

Vision

To be South Africa's leading academy for pet care and training, building a compassionate nation where every animal is valued.

Our Mission

Pawsitive Pet Academy exists to empower pet owners, families, aspiring professionals, and future animal caregivers through expert-led education.

Our Goals

  • To educate
  • Equip pet owners
  • Aspiring professionals with proper skills
  • To create meaningful employmenet
  • To create business opportunities for individaul who are passionate about working with animals
  • To deliver a seamless, user centred digita experience through the website and mobile applicaton
  • To connect more leaners with life-changing courses
